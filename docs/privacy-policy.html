Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

Introduction

Japanese Trainer ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

Data Stored Locally

The app stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Your learning preferences (selected level, difficulty, language)
  • Training statistics (words practiced, success rates, points)
  • Notification settings (enabled/disabled, reminder time)

This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to any server.

Advertising

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect:

  • Device identifiers
  • IP address
  • Usage data for ad personalization

You can opt out of personalized ads in your device settings. See Google's Privacy Policy for more details.

In-App Purchases

Premium subscriptions are processed through Google Play. We do not collect or store any payment information. See Google's Privacy Policy for payment data handling.

How We Use Your Information

Local data is used solely to:

  • Save your learning progress
  • Personalize your training experience
  • Send local notification reminders (if enabled)

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal data with third parties, except:

  • Google AdMob for advertising purposes
  • Google Play for subscription management

Data Security

Your data is stored locally on your device using standard secure storage mechanisms. We do not have access to this data.

Children's Privacy

The app is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app does not require any personal information to function.

Your Rights

You can:

  • Delete all app data by clearing the app's storage in your device settings
  • Uninstall the app to remove all local data
  • Opt out of personalized ads in your device's Google settings

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
